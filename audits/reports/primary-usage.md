# `primary` usage inventory (prompt 016)

Every `primary` colour class in `resources/views` on `main` before the change, classified as **text**, a
**fill under text**, or **decoration**, and what happened to it on `ui/primary-strong-contrast`.
Generated by script from `git show main:<file>` and checked by hand; the structural guard
`tests/Unit/Architecture/PrimaryIsNotUsedForTextTest.php` keeps it true.

**Rule (option B):** `primary` `#008fe6` (3.46:1 on white) is the accent — rules, borders, focus rings, icons,
checkbox accents, icon-only fills (non-text needs 3:1) and large display text ≥24px (3:1). Anything read at
normal size, and any fill under text, is `primary-strong` `#0078cc` (4.61:1 on white; white on it 4.61:1).

Also in CSS: `--ring` and `.heading-rule::after` use `--primary` (focus ring, accent rule: decoration, unchanged).
